About

Product thinking from the editorial side of the room.

My background spans marketing, editorial operations, and live production. I learned to approach software the same way I approach a signal chain: understand the system, find the failure, and make the result legible to the person using it.

My current work sits around browser-native software, local-first workflows, provenance, creative tools, and human/AI interaction. I get a real artifact on screen quickly, test the experience hard, and refuse to let decorative complexity stand in for functionality.

I’m based on the East Side of Providence and work well asynchronously with founders, small teams, researchers, artists, and people carrying a difficult idea that needs a clearer container.
