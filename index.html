    The operating sequence

    Preserve. Extract. Reconstruct. Verify.

    ChronosAudit is not a chatbot laid over a folder. It is a controlled transformation pipeline in which every derived object retains its inputs and every uncertainty remains inspectable.

    01

    Preserve

    Inventory original bytes, compute integrity hashes, record metadata, and prevent downstream tools from mutating the source corpus.

    02

    Extract

    Capture explicit dates, email headers, embedded metadata, relative references, and source locations without treating them as interchangeable.

    03

    Reconstruct

    Cluster assertions into events, compare versions, and surface changes, omissions, contradictions, and unresolved sequences.

    04

    Verify

    Move backward from any timeline event to the exact claim, highlighted passage, original file, and recorded processing run.

    Built for evidence-heavy work

    When sequence and source matter as much as content.

    ChronosAudit is intended for people who need to explain not only what a corpus says, but when a claim appeared, how it changed, and whether the supporting record agrees.

    Investigations

    Reconstruct decision paths across emails, notices, drafts, attachments, and public statements.

    Legal and discovery

    Separate document dates, metadata dates, stated dates, and later interpretations before chronology hardens into narrative.

    Historical research

    Trace changing terminology and claims across a fragmented private or institutional archive.

    Corporate review

    Identify when requirements, approvals, commitments, or ownership changed across revisions.

    Private corpus, local instrument

    Your investigation should not become somebody else’s dataset.

    The canonical application is designed to process real corpora on the investigator’s own device. The public website uses a bundled synthetic case so a visitor can understand the product without selecting a private file or trusting an unexplained upload boundary.
