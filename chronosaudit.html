What it proves

A SHA-256 fingerprint identifies a precise byte sequence. Matching hashes are strong evidence that two files are byte-for-byte identical.

What stays local

Selected file contents, hashing, persistence, and comparison happen in your browser. Exports contain metadata and hashes, not the files themselves.

What it cannot prove

A manifest alone does not establish authorship, truth, custody, or meaning. It is a durable identification layer for a larger evidence workflow.
