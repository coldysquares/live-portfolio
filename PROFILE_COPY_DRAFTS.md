## Fiverr gig 1 — Product and UX audit

### Title

**I will audit your AI product, browser app, or prototype and give you a concrete repair plan**

### Opening

Your product may look finished while still feeling confusing, untrustworthy, slow, fake, or difficult to explain. I will review the real experience, identify the highest-impact failures, and give you a prioritized plan for what to fix next.

### Good fit

- AI products and chat interfaces
- Browser tools and interactive demos
- Early SaaS or internal tools
- Portfolio products that need to become credible
- Apps with dead controls, unclear claims, or weak conversion paths

### Deliverables

- Direct usability and product diagnosis
- Concrete bugs, dead ends, and trust failures
- Review of claims versus actual functionality
- Prioritized repair list
- Copy and positioning notes where the interface overpromises or confuses
- Optional annotated screenshots or implementation notes, depending on scope

### Client requirements

Please send the live link or preview, the intended user, the action you most want them to take, and any area you already suspect is failing.

### FAQ

**Will you just give general design advice?**  
No. The useful output is specific: what is wrong, where it occurs, why it matters, and what to do next.

**Can you review an unfinished prototype?**  
Yes. Clearly identifying the current boundary is part of the work.

**Will you modify the product?**  
That can be scoped separately after the audit. The audit itself should remain useful even if another developer implements the changes.

## Fiverr gig 2 — Working browser prototype

### Title

**I will build a focused working browser prototype for your product idea**

### Opening

Before you fund a large build, prove the interaction. I will turn one bounded product idea into a working browser prototype that you can click, test, show, and learn from.

### Good fit

- Local-first tools
- File, text, audio, or data workflows
- Interactive explainers and visualizations
- AI-assisted interfaces with a clear model or API boundary
- Internal tools and workflow proofs
- Unusual interface ideas that need a real artifact

### Deliverables

- One clearly defined browser workflow
- Responsive interface
- Real browser functionality for the agreed operation
- Honest prototype and capability labeling
- Source files and a concise implementation note
- Optional Vercel-ready static deployment structure

### Boundary

This is for a focused proof, not an undefined full SaaS platform. We agree on the one interaction that must work before the build starts.

### Client requirements

Please describe the user, the input, the operation, the output, and what the prototype must prove. Screenshots, rough sketches, and ugly existing versions are welcome.

### FAQ

**Can the prototype use AI?**  
Yes, when a real model or API supports the agreed function. I do not disguise templates or random output as AI.

**Can it run locally in the browser?**  
Often. The exact boundary depends on file size, browser support, model weight, and the operation involved.

**Will it be production-ready?**  
The agreed workflow will be real and testable. Authentication, billing, large-scale storage, compliance, and heavy backend infrastructure are separate scopes unless explicitly included.

## Fiverr gig 3 — Structural editing

### Title

**I will structurally edit your thesis, executive document, or research-heavy draft**

### Opening

I will help you make a difficult document clearer without flattening the thinking that makes it yours. This is structural and developmental editing for drafts that need stronger organization, argument flow, clarity, and reader guidance.

### Good fit

- Theses and dissertations
- Artist statements and research statements
- Executive and board documents
- Long reports and white papers
- Research-heavy essays
- Documents assembled from multiple contributors

### Deliverables

Depending on scope:

- Structural and argument-level edit
- Section and sequence recommendations
- Clarity and redundancy pass
- Comments on unsupported leaps or missing transitions
- Style-guide alignment review
- Clean edited file plus an editorial memo

### Boundary

I will improve structure, clarity, and presentation. I will not fabricate sources, invent findings, or complete academic work that must represent the author’s own scholarship.

### Client requirements

Please send the document, target audience, required style guide, deadline, current word count, and the type of feedback you have already received.

## Commercial guardrails

- Price from defined scope, length, complexity, and turnaround.
- Do not promise a full product under the label “prototype.”
- Do not sell fake AI functionality or unverifiable automation.
- Make the buyer’s input, the performed operation, and the delivered artifact explicit.
- Use a small paid diagnostic or audit when the correct implementation scope is not yet knowable.
