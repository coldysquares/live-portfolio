Product direction

Source first. Inference second.

The larger idea is simple: preserve the originals, extract what can be known, make uncertainty visible, and let every conclusion lead back to the material that supports it. The public browser demo proves the local evidence-handling layer; the broader reconstruction workflow is still being built.
